About Urban Haven

Your Home Away From Home

12+ Years of Excellence

Urban Haven Apartments has proudly served the Murang'a, Kiharu area for over 12 years, offering reliable, high-quality rental spaces that feel like home. Over the years, we've built a strong reputation for providing safe, welcoming, and well-maintained apartments that suit the nedds of students, professionals, and families alike.

Our goal is to offer more than just a place to stay- we strive to create a living environment that is comfortable, affordable, and supportive. We understand that home is where you recharge, grow and build memories, which is why our spaces are designed with both function and comfort in mind.

With various apartment options, modern amenities like reliable internet, clean water, and secure surroundings, plus a responsive management team, Urban Haven ensures your stay is smooth and enjoyable.
